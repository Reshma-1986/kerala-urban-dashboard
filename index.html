Urban Growth in Kerala: 2000 vs 2020

About Kerala

  • Location: Southwestern India, along the Malabar Coast
  • Area: 38,863 km²
  • Population (2021 est.): ~35 million
  • Urbanization rate: ~47%
  • Number of districts: 14
  • Notable: High literacy, strong human development indicators, rapid urban expansion

Dataset Source

The urban extent datasets for 2000 and 2020 are derived from the Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL) WUP (World Settlement Population) data. These datasets classify urban areas based on built-up presence and population density.
